How is my GWA computed? ▾

Four Dean's List Criteria

  1. GWA Rule: General Weighted Average must be ≤ {{ dl_config.max_gwa }}, calculated over credit subjects only with numerical grades (sum of units × grade divided by credit units).
  2. Lowest Grade Rule: No single subject grade lower than {{ dl_config.worst_allowed_grade }} (grades 2.5, 2.75, 3.0, and 5.0 disqualify).
  3. No 5.0, INC, or IP Rule: Must not have any failing mark (5.0), incomplete mark (INC), or in-progress mark (IP).
  4. Full Load Rule: Total units (credit plus non-credit subjects such as NSTP) must meet or exceed your prescribed semester load.
